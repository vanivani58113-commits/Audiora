Project Overview

About Audiora

Bridging historical preservation, accessible speech technologies, and modern interactive web design.

Discover Heritage Through Sound

Audiora – Heritage Audio Guide was designed as an organized educational web application to make historical learning intuitive, enjoyable, and accessible. In traditional classrooms and museums, historical facts are often dry texts; Audiora transforms them into multi-sensory experiences by pairing realistic historical facts with voice synthesis and interactive discovery.

  • Web SpeechSynthesis Audio API

    Native browser speech synthesis allows every user to hear natural audio narrations without external heavy media streaming dependencies.

  • Realistic & Verified Historical Content

    Curated architectural facts, imperial builder records (Chola, Pallava, Mughal, Ganga dynasties), and authentic construction techniques.

  • Fully Responsive & Accessible

    Engineered with semantic HTML5, fluid CSS grid, and mobile-first responsiveness ensuring equal usability on smartphones, tablets, and laptops.
